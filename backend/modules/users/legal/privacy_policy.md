# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data RealChat ("RealChat", "we", "us", or "our") collects when you use the RealChat Telegram bot and its related web pages (together, the "Service"), why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. It forms part of our User Agreement.

## 1. Who we are

RealChat operates the Service and decides how and why your personal data is processed. For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, use the support contact shown in the bot's /help and /paysupport.

## 2. Our privacy commitments

In short, and as explained in detail in the rest of this Privacy Policy:

- We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data.
- We do not share your personal data with anyone for their own purposes. It is handled by Telegram and our hosting provider to run the Service, and disclosed to others only as described in section 5: where the law requires it, or where necessary to prevent serious harm, investigate abuse, or protect our rights or the safety of users.
- We do not show advertising, and we do not use third-party analytics, tracking, or cookies.
- We do not use your messages to train artificial intelligence models.
- We do not keep your data forever. Messages are deleted automatically after a limited period, and your profile is erased as soon as you delete your account. Only the minimal records described in section 8.4 are kept after that, for safety, accounting, and legal reasons.
- Other users never see your Telegram name, username, or phone number.
- We never download the photos, videos, or files you send; we store only Telegram's reference to them.

## 3. The data we collect

### 3.1 Data from your Telegram account
We receive and store your Telegram user ID, which identifies your account. We do not store your Telegram name, username, or phone number, and other users never see them.

### 3.2 Your profile
Your nickname, age, gender, and country, and any city, bio, and profile photo you add. For a photo, we store only Telegram's file identifier; we never download the image itself. We also keep a short history of changes to your age and gender, to apply the limits on how often they can be changed.

### 3.3 Your chats and messages
Who you were matched with and when each chat started and ended. The messages you send in chats: their text or caption, any edits, and Telegram's file identifier for media (we never download the files), with the time they were sent, and whether they were sent in Private mode or as view-once media.

### 3.4 Direct messages and chat requests
Direct messages you send or receive from profile cards (their text or caption, any formatting, and Telegram's file identifier for media), with when they were delivered and opened. Chat requests you send or receive, and whether they were accepted or declined.

### 3.5 Interactions with other users
Profile cards you visit and who visits yours, likes you give and receive, blocks you make and receive, reports you make and receive (including any details you write in a report), and activity alerts you set or that others set about you.

### 3.6 Moderation
The outcome of reports you make or receive, and any warnings, suspensions, and bans on your account, with the rule concerned and the dates. We also record each time a moderator or admin looks up your account or opens your stored messages, and an admin's reason for opening them.

### 3.7 Coins and purchases
Your coin balance and every change to it, holds on coins for features in progress, and your purchases: what you bought, the price in Telegram Stars, Telegram's charge identifier, whether a purchase was refunded, and your VIP end date. Payments are handled by Telegram; we never receive your bank or card details.

### 3.8 Activity and settings
When you last used the Service, and the days on which you used it (counted by India date; your RealChat activity, never your Telegram online status), your notification and privacy settings, the versions of this Privacy Policy and our User Agreement you accepted and when, your invite link and who joined through it, and, while daily rewards are offered, when you claim them, to time reminders.

### 3.9 Technical data
Our servers keep short-lived technical data to run the Service, such as which step of setup you are on and protection against message flooding. Our logs record events and errors without message content.

## 4. How we use your data and why

We use your data to:

- create your account and profile, and show your profile card to the people the Service allows to see it;
- match you with other users, relay your messages, and provide features such as direct messages, chat requests, likes, visits, and activity alerts;
- keep the Service safe: detect and prevent abuse, review reports, and enforce our User Agreement;
- process purchases, manage coins and VIP, and handle refunds and support;
- send you notifications you have not turned off, such as visit, like, and reminder messages;
- keep records we need for accounting, security, and to comply with the law; and
- understand how the Service is used, as totals only (such as how many people sign up or use it each day), and maintain and improve it.

We process your data on the basis of the consent you give when you tap "Continue", for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, and, where applicable law allows, for legitimate uses such as complying with the law, responding to legal requests, and protecting the safety of users. We use your data only for these purposes.

Before you tap "Continue", we store only what is needed to show you this Privacy Policy and our User Agreement and to record your acceptance: your Telegram user ID, an empty profile with your RealChat ID, the step of setup you are on, and, if you started RealChat through an invite link, who invited you. If you do not continue, you can ask us to delete it through the support contact shown in the bot's /help.

Some of this processing is automatic: matching pairs users without anyone choosing who you meet, and automatic filters block links, contact details, and flooding in chats. Decisions to warn, suspend, or ban an account are made by people, our moderators and admins, from a report or their own review. A suspension ends by itself at the end of its period.

## 5. Who can see your data

### 5.1 Other users
Other users see only what the Service shows them: your profile card (nickname, age, gender, country, and any city, bio, photo, and RealChat ID), the messages you send them, and, where the feature applies, that you visited or liked their profile, sent them a direct message, or sent them a chat request. People you block or report are only told that a chat ended.

### 5.2 Our moderators and admins
Our moderators and admins see what they need to keep the Service safe. When they review a report, they can see the profile cards of the people involved, the account's moderation record, and the content concerned: the stored messages of the reported chat from both people, including Private mode messages, edits, and media, or, for a report about a profile, the direct messages and chat requests between the two people. They can look up an account by its RealChat ID and see its profile card, status, moderation record, and the reports it made and received. Only admins can open an account's stored chats and direct messages outside a report, and only after recording a reason, to investigate abuse, handle an appeal, or comply with the law. Photos and other media are shown to them by having our bot send a copy to their own Telegram chat, protected from forwarding and saving in Telegram apps that honour that setting, which our bot then tries to delete about 10 minutes later. Every look-up and every opening of stored messages or media is recorded in our audit log. They never see your Telegram name, username, or phone number.

### 5.3 Service providers
- Telegram: every message and button press passes through Telegram, which processes it under its own privacy policy. Purchases with Telegram Stars are processed by Telegram.
- Hosting: our servers and databases are hosted on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure in India.

### 5.4 Legal requirements
We may disclose data where we are required to by law, by a court order, or by a lawful request from a government authority, or where it is necessary to prevent serious harm, investigate abuse, or protect our rights or the safety of users.

### 5.5 Transfer of the Service
If the Service is transferred to another operator, your data may be transferred with it, subject to this Privacy Policy.

## 6. Information you share with other users

What you tell other users in chats, direct messages, your bio, or your photo is shared by your own choice and with your consent. Once it is delivered, we cannot control what the recipient does with it, and we cannot remove it from their Telegram account or devices. Deleting your account or your messages from RealChat does not delete copies held by other users. This Privacy Policy covers how RealChat handles your data, not how other users handle what you send them; they are responsible for that. To the fullest extent permitted by law, we are not responsible for personal information you choose to share with other users, or for how they use it. Think carefully before sharing anything that could identify you.

## 7. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on our servers in India. Telegram processes messages and payments on its own infrastructure, which may be located in other countries, under its own privacy policy.

We may move the Service and your data to different servers or hosting providers at any time, without notice, and the Service may be unavailable while we do. Your data stays protected as this Privacy Policy describes, and if a move changes the provider or the country where your data is stored, we will update this Privacy Policy.

## 8. How long we keep your data

### 8.1 Messages
Chat messages and direct messages are deleted automatically after a retention period that we set and may change. Messages that are part of a report still under review are kept until the review ends.

### 8.2 Visits and past chats
Profile visits are deleted automatically after a set period. Past chats are listed for a set period after they end. Records of the days you used the Service are deleted after about 400 days.

### 8.3 Your profile and account
Your profile and settings are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, we immediately erase your profile (including your nickname, age, gender, country, city, bio, photo, change history, and RealChat ID), your coins, any VIP time, your settings, your past chats list and visitors, the record of the days you used the Service, your invite link, and your acceptances of our terms.

### 8.4 What we keep after deletion
After deletion, we keep a minimal record (your Telegram user ID, account status, and dates) so that restrictions cannot be avoided by signing up again, together with blocks and reports you made or received, warnings, suspensions, and bans on your account, records of coin changes and purchases needed for accounting and to handle payments and refunds, and records of administrative actions, including the audit log of who looked up accounts or opened stored messages. A suspension or ban stays in force after deletion. Messages are still deleted at the end of their retention period.

## 9. How we protect your data

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including limiting access to the data and systems to the people who need it, and connecting to Telegram over encrypted connections. We will never ask you for your Telegram login code, password, or payment details. However, messages are not end-to-end encrypted, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your data. You are responsible for the security of your own Telegram account and devices. If a data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

## 10. Cookies and tracking

The Service does not use cookies, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics. The web pages where this Privacy Policy and our User Agreement are published load no scripts and set no cookies.

## 11. Your choices and rights

### 11.1 Choices in the Service
You can edit your profile at any time with /profile, turn notifications and activity alerts about you on or off in Advanced settings, use Private mode in chats, block other users, and delete your account at any time.

### 11.2 Your rights
Subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

- get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
- have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected and updated;
- have your data erased, which you can do yourself by deleting your account;
- withdraw your consent at any time, by deleting your account (this does not affect processing that took place before);
- nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to; and
- have your complaint heard by our Grievance Officer, and, if you are not satisfied with the outcome, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

### 11.3 How to make a request
Send your request through the support contact shown in the bot's /help and /paysupport. We may ask you to confirm the request from your Telegram account. We will acknowledge it within 24 hours and aim to respond within 15 days.
